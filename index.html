A useful sequence

Four checks before checkout.

Use these habits when researching BaseTao finds or comparing listings from any fashion catalog.

01 / DISCOVER

Choose the exact version

Record the product link, seller, color and selected variant. Similar photographs can belong to different listings.

02 / MEASURE

Compare with what fits

Use a familiar garment or shoe as your reference. Check the seller’s measuring method before comparing numbers.

03 / INSPECT

Ask a specific question

A clear request for a seam close-up or width measurement is more useful than asking whether an item is “good.”

04 / PLAN

Review the complete cost

Keep product cost, delivery, service charges and potential import charges separate. Leave unknown amounts unfilled.

Fashion sizing guide

Measure for the category.

There is no universal fit across brands or sellers. Start with measurements and add the detail that matters for the piece.

What to record before adding a listing to your shortlist
Category Compare first Ask before ordering
Shoes Foot length, the seller’s size chart and a familiar shoe’s fit. Does the measurement mean foot length, recommended foot length or insole length?
Hoodies / Sweaters Chest width, body length, sleeve length and intended ease. What is the fabric composition, and are care instructions supplied?
T-Shirts Shoulder width, chest width and length measured flat. Are the measurements taken before washing, and is shrinkage information available?
Jackets Chest, shoulders and sleeve length with room for your usual layers. Which performance claims are supported by material specifications?
Pants / Shorts Waist, rise, thigh width and inseam against a pair you own. Is the waistband measurement relaxed or stretched?
Headwear Head circumference, band width and adjustment range. Is the closure adjustable, and what dimensions are provided?
Sets Separate top and bottom measurements. Can different sizes be selected, and exactly which pieces are included?
Underwear / Underpants Waist, rise, fabric composition and seam placement. What hygiene-related return restrictions apply?
Jersey Chest, length, sleeve shape and intended layering. Can you inspect print placement and confirm the care instructions?
Accessories Dimensions, material description, fastening and intended use. For jewelry, is the metal composition documented and suitable for your sensitivities?
The reading desk

Three guides for a clearer decision.

Guide 01 / Organizing finds

Build a comparison sheet that answers real questions

For a beginner’s BaseTao spreadsheet, start with product name, source URL, selected variant, seller, measurements, observed cost, date checked and unresolved questions. One row should describe one specific option. Give a different color or size its own row when its details differ.

Keep item cost, domestic delivery, service charges, international freight and possible destination charges in separate fields. A listing price by itself cannot describe the delivered cost. Record the currency and the date rather than presenting an old amount as current.

Product link tracking also needs a status. Use “researching,” “waiting for information” or “ready to reconsider” with a short reason. Mark missing information as unknown; a blank measurement is a reason to ask, rather than an invitation to assume.

Before ordering: reopen the source listing and confirm the variant, availability, seller terms and measurements. A saved row is a research record, not a reservation.

Guide 02 / Reading evidence

Read QC photos as a set of observations

A useful warehouse photo checklist starts with the exact variant recorded in your notes. Compare its visible shape, labels and included accessories with the listing. For stitching inspection, examine seams, panel joins and edges instead of judging the whole item from a thumbnail.

  • Fit evidence: request a clear measurement photo when a size label is insufficient.
  • Color evidence: account for lighting, camera processing and screen differences.
  • Construction evidence: ask for a close-up of an unclear seam, fastening or hardware attachment.
  • Missing details: name the exact angle or feature that needs another photograph.

Write an observation such as “chest measurement still unclear” instead of an unsupported score. Photos alone do not establish authenticity, composition or long-term durability. For branded items, seller provenance and appropriate authentication require separate evidence.

Guide 03 / Parcel planning

Plan the parcel before the shortlist becomes an order

Record item weight, expected packaging and destination requirements early. A jacket’s volume and a shoe box can affect a parcel differently from a small accessory. Carriers may calculate charges using actual or volumetric weight under route-specific rules; confirm the applicable calculation.

Compare shoe box packing decisions with the protection you need. Removing packaging can reduce volume in some cases, but may increase damage risk or remove information you wanted to keep. Ask for final packed dimensions before relying on an estimate.

For UK shopping cost checks, EU import charge checks or US import requirement checks, use current destination guidance for the item and shipment. Country rules, carrier services and handling charges can differ; one shopper’s past total is not your quote.

Track two deadlines separately: the seller’s return window and any warehouse storage deadline. Storage time does not automatically extend return eligibility.

Direct answers

Before you follow a link.

The essentials about this page, sizing, evidence and shipping estimates.

What is a BaseTao spreadsheet?

A BaseTao spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds, source links, variants, measurements and buying notes. The term can refer to a sheet or a browsing guide; this page is an editorial guide with links to CAFANS categories.

Can I download a spreadsheet or order on this page?

This page provides reading guides and category links, rather than a downloadable spreadsheet or checkout. Category buttons open CAFANS; verify each destination listing and its ordering options there.

How should I compare clothing and sneaker sizes?

Compare the listing’s measurements with a similar item that already fits. Check the measuring method and units. For footwear, distinguish foot length, recommended foot length and measured insole length; they are not interchangeable.

Do QC photos prove that a branded item is authentic?

No. QC photos can document visible condition and details, but cannot establish authenticity on their own. Check seller provenance, supporting documentation and appropriate independent authentication before relying on an authenticity claim.

Why can the final shipping cost differ from an early estimate?

Packed dimensions, actual weight, the selected route and optional services can change the charge. Confirm the current carrier calculation and final packing details before approving shipment.

Is this an official BaseTao or brand website?

No. This is an independent editorial page. It does not claim affiliation with BaseTao, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or other brands. CAFANS links are browsing destinations, not evidence of official brand authorization.
